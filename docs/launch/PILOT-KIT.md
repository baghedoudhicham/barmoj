## Moderator guide

Before a session, agree its format with the adult and confirm the support contact. Use the family's own browser/device for real activity; use a separate preview and invented text for demonstrations. Do not retain recordings, screenshots or copies of real children's answers. If a call is used, agree it with the adult and keep recording off; no automated child transcript.

Opening: “We are testing whether this activity is clear. We are not testing how smart your child is. You may pause or stop whenever you want.” Confirm adult agreement and ask whether the child wants to try. This is a facilitation prompt, not a verified guardian identity or a substitute for a reviewed research/consent process.

1. Let the child choose a lab. Ask: “What do you think you need to know first?” Observe whether the goal and controls are understandable.
2. Ask for a prediction before running the test. Do not supply the answer. Drawing, pointing or oral explanation is welcome; an adult may type the child's own words into the required fields if the child chooses that support. Mark adult scribing in the private tracker.
3. Run the test and ask: “What happened? What surprised you?” A mistaken prediction is useful evidence, not a penalty.
4. Invite one change and a retest. Ask: “What did you change, and why?” Follow the model's completion requirements; do not imply a single successful run completes all evidence gates.
5. Ask for a short explanation and one transfer example. Do not score it as intelligence or mastery.
6. Open the Parent Dashboard together. Ask the adult to explain what the record represents and where it is stored. Clarify shared-browser access and local deletion.
7. Finish: “What should we make clearer?” Ask the adult whether a voluntary return would be useful, without pressure or incentives tied to performance.

Budget roughly 15–20 minutes for introduction, one lab and discussion, adjusting to the family's needs. This is a moderator scheduling estimate, not a child timer or completion requirement. Stop if the child wishes; an incomplete session is a useful observation.

Record barrier categories such as unclear goal, reading load, control discovery, prediction entry, result interpretation, explanation entry, evidence understanding or device issue. Describe interface problems without labels about the child. Review the first six sessions before expanding to ten.

## Four demonstration production scripts

Use invented answers and a clean preview browser. Each clip is a finite 9:16 demonstration, roughly 30–40 seconds. Use an adult voice, readable static captions, no rapid cuts and no music that obscures speech. Review the actual recording for crop, numbers, Arabic direction, exact model outputs and caption timing. Do not show a real child or claim a learning outcome from demonstration footage.

| Clip | Opening | Before → after to show | Closing parent prompt |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Water | ماذا لو أخطأ الحساس؟ | With the 80% stopping rule, true water 96% and sensor 60%, one sensor leaves the pump running. Enable the backup sensor and retest: the pump stops. For the full lab evidence, also test ordinary 45% and boundary 80% with the corrected rule. | اسأل طفلك: ما المعلومة الثانية التي نحتاجها؟ |
| Routing | ماذا نغيّر إذا أُغلق الطريق؟ | Start A–B–C with A first. Close A–B: the same order violates the route constraint. Change to A–C–B and retest: priority is preserved and B is reached through C. Keep Latin stop labels visually unambiguous. | اسأل طفلك: كيف حافظنا على الهدف وغيّرنا الخطة؟ |
| Traffic | ماذا لو طلب الاتجاهان المرور؟ | Both green requests without interlock produce two green outputs. Add the interlock and retest the conflicting request: both outputs become red. A normal one-direction test still allows only one direction. | اسأل طفلك: هل كل طلب يجب أن ينفّذه النظام؟ |
| Economy | هل المكافأة الأكبر تجعل القاعدة أفضل دائمًا؟ | Start 10 resources, cost 3, reward 5: five rounds end at 20. Change reward to 3: five rounds keep the balance at 10. Show the actual tested chart, not an invented trend. | اسأل طفلك: ما العلاقة بين التكلفة والمكافأة؟ |

Suggested pacing: first 5 seconds pose the question; next 10 show the prediction and original test; next 10 show one change and retest; final 5–10 explain the evidence and invite the adult to one lab. Extend the clip if captions need more reading time. Avoid implying that the abbreviated video shows the entire completion sequence.

Final slate in each language:

- Arabic: **جرّبوا مختبرًا واحدًا معًا. تجربة مجانية بإشراف بالغ.**
- English: **Try one lab together. A free, adult-supervised pilot.**
- French: **Essayez un atelier ensemble. Un pilote gratuit, accompagné par un adulte.**

Include the current Firebase URL and the true availability: **المنهج وواجهة الأسرة وأربعة مختبرات تفاعلية بالعربية والإنجليزية والفرنسية** / **The curriculum, family pages and four interactive labs are available in Arabic, English and French** / **Le parcours, les pages familiales et quatre ateliers interactifs sont disponibles en arabe, anglais et français**. Four cards, with invented demonstration text only:

2. **قبل التجربة: ماذا توقعت، ولماذا؟** Show one invented prediction.

4. **الأمانة أن نصف ما حدث، والإحسان أن نحسّن ما نبنيه.** Invite an adult to try one lab and discuss the evidence. No religious quotations, piety scores or cognitive benefit claims.

## Measurement sheet handling

Copy `pilot-session-tracker-template.csv` to a private, access-controlled operational workspace. The repository contains the blank schema only. Never commit a completed sheet, adult contact list or child answers.

Use random family codes unrelated to initials or email. `market` is an optional broad country label reported in aggregates; no city or precise location. Keep permissioned adult contacts separately, with an owner and a chosen retention/deletion date before collection. Do not record religion, diagnosis, exact age, school, photo, voice, full name or verbatim explanation.

Allowed fields: language `ar/en/fr/mixed`; device `phone/tablet/desktop`; support `independent/adult_scribed/adult_guidance/not_observed`; yes/no fields may be `unknown` or blank rather than guessed. `explanation_observed` records whether the child attempted an explanation, not whether the platform judged it correct. `return_7d` is a voluntary adult report, not a tracked event.

Report invited → attended → completed with exact denominators, voluntary return among families with a seven-day opportunity, parent understanding and moderator minutes. Exclude future/unknown return observations from the return denominator and explain that choice. Treat counts as usability signals, not evidence of increased intelligence or cured attention difficulties.
