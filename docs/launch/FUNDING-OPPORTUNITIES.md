# BRKAR funding and programme scan

Checked 10 October 2026. This is an opportunity shortlist, not a determination of eligibility. Public programme pages and call dates can change; verify with the operator before preparing or submitting an application.

## Best route to qualify for next

**Tamwilcom Innov Invest — Tech Start.** This is the closest public funding instrument for an early product build: it targets startups past proof of concept that are developing a prototype or MVP, after validation by a Tamwilcom partner. The grant covers up to 80% of eligible costs, capped at MAD 200,000 (MAD 400,000 for a project with an invention patent), with a 20% founder contribution. Eligible categories include product and market-development work, staff, hosting and marketing. Application is through a partner structure; this is not a direct self-serve grant. Ask an incubation partner whether a current intake is open and whether BRKAR's current maturity qualifies. [Official Tech Start terms](https://www.tamwilstartups.ma/techStart.php) · [Tamwilcom partner list](https://tamwilstartups.ma/partenaires.php)

**What to establish before applying:** whether BRKAR is incorporated under Moroccan law, who owns it and resides in Morocco, whether the current app counts as a validated POC/MVP, the intended six-month budget and contribution, and what product milestones the grant would fund. The live product is useful evidence of execution, but it is not user traction or proof of learning impact. Keep the pitch evidence-based: supervised pilot recruitment, observed family sessions, language review, privacy and safeguarding work, and a clear path to sustainable revenue.

## Follow for the next intake

**Startup Venture Building (Startup VB).** MTNRA and Tamwilcom describe a national pathway with incubation and acceleration support, including life and incubation grants, honor loans and seed loans; the government also names EdTech among sectors for specialist programmes. Operators listed by Tamwilcom include Technopark, Open Startup International, Renew Capital, Flat6Labs, CEED Morocco and 500 Global. The 2026 calls identified in this scan have closed: Ignite Morocco (Flat6Labs, deadline extended to 7 August), The Builders (CEED, 20 July), TechnoVentures (Technopark, 16 July), Open Startup Pre-Seed (6 September), and Sanabil by 500 Global (9 August). TechnoVentures' date is from a secondary tracker; confirm any future intake with Technopark. No next cohort date was confirmed on the official pages checked. Ask the operators about the next round rather than applying through an expired form. [MTNRA Startup offer](https://www.mmsp.gov.ma/fr/nos-metiers/startups) · [Tamwilcom VB operators](https://tamwilstartups.ma/partenairesSVB.php) · [Flat6Labs deadline update](https://fr.linkedin.com/posts/flat6labs_mtnra-marocdigital2030-startupvb-activity-7489278018352353280-DQUw) · [CEED Morocco call](https://ma.linkedin.com/company/ceed-maroc) · [500 Global Sanabil](https://mena.500.co/founders/mena/seed-accelerator) · [Open Startup Pre-Seed eligibility and dates](https://svb.open-startup.org/) · [The Pulse 2026 call tracker](https://www.thepulse.ma/ressources?category=Appels+%C3%A0+projets)

**Tamwilcom Innov Invest — Tech Boost.** This is a later-stage option, not the first ask. It targets a startup beyond MVP that is improving the product and going to market to reach product-market fit. The honor loan covers up to 80% of eligible expenses, normally capped at MAD 500,000 (MAD 750,000 for disruptive DeepTech), with a 20% contribution. It is interest-free and unsecured, with repayment up to five years and a possible grace period up to two years. BRKAR should revisit it after real pilot feedback and early customer evidence. [Official Tech Boost terms](https://www.tamwilstartups.ma/techBoost.php)

## One near-term event to verify, not count as grant funding

**Moroccan Sahara Innovation Challenge 2026 (UCA).** The university announcement says applications run through 18 October and accepts startups in a SCALE track, for solutions tied to the Southern Provinces. However, the challenge portal currently displays “applications are closed.” The event also requires in-person participation in Marrakech and Laâyoune (26–31 October). Selected projects may get a year-long maturation path; the public pages reviewed do not promise a cash grant amount. Apply only if BRKAR has a genuine, specific use case for the target territories and the organizers confirm that applications are still being accepted. [UCA call announcement](https://www.uca.ma/en/news/cadi-ayyad-university-launches-the-moroccan-sahara-innovation-challenge-2026-call-for-applications) · [Challenge portal and status](https://msic.uca.ma/)

- **Orange Summer Challenge:** 2026 theme is AI business solutions; the listed application deadline was 20 June, so this cycle is closed. Keep Orange Digital Center / Orange Fab on the relationship list for future commercial or acceleration opportunities, rather than treating them as an open grant now. [Orange Summer Challenge](https://osc.gos.orange.com/) · [Orange Fab Morocco overview](https://engageforchange.orange.com/en/programs/ff22195d-384d-4796-bf19-566550e25be0)
- **POESAM:** the 2026 call closed on 17 May. The digital-impact fit may be worth reconsidering next year after BRKAR has measured evidence from family pilots. [Orange POESAM 2026](https://www.orange.com/fr/nos-actualites/jeunes-entrepreneurs-dafrique-et-moyen-orient-candidatez-au-poesam-2026)

[ISTITMAR criteria](https://marocpme.gov.ma/istitmar/istitmar-pme/) · [TATWIR scope](https://marocpme.gov.ma/tatwir/)

## Next practical steps

1. Confirm legal entity, ownership/residency, founder team and current POC/MVP stage.
2. Contact one or two Tamwilcom-listed incubation partners (for example StartUp Maroc or Emerging Business Factory) with a concise one-page BRKAR brief and ask about current Tech Start intake, the required proof-of-concept evidence, eligible spending, and the 20% contribution. These are candidate conversations, not endorsements or confirmed acceptances.
3. Follow Tamwilcom's operator pages for the next Startup VB cohort; compare the eligibility and stage before choosing a programme. Do not frame the product as an AI startup unless the product actually contains a bounded, validated AI capability.
4. Build the case from adult-supervised trial evidence. Do not publish child stories, answers or identifiable data in an application deck; use consented, de-identified adult feedback only.
