# BRKAR organic launch content pack

Prepared 7 October 2026. These are adult-facing drafts for organic review, not published posts or ads. Use only after the operator/support details and pilot safeguards are ready. Keep the working Firebase URL until domain ownership is confirmed. Do not add pixels, child data, testimonials or paid promotion to this first pass.

## Editorial rule

Show one question, one test and one reason to talk together. Speak to the parent, not to a child as a consumer. Let each result remain readable. A clip or card should not promise improved attention, intelligence or school performance. The product is a finite set of four interactive labs, not an endless feed or an adaptive AI tutor.

All drafts below point to the current live site. The landing page, family setup, Kid Home, privacy page, Parent Dashboard, curriculum, lab catalogue and four interactive labs are available in Arabic, English and French. Fluent review and real-device QA remain before publication.

## Four-week organic sequence

| Week | Format | Theme | Adult action |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 9:16 quiet screen demonstration | Water sensor: what if one reading is wrong? | Try the water lab together and ask what second evidence would help. |
| 2 | Four-card carousel | Predict, test, explain | Choose one lab and let the child make the first prediction. |
| 3 | 9:16 screen demonstration | A plan can change while its goal stays | Ask what changed in the route and what stayed the same. |
| 4 | Static parent-facing post or short screen capture | What this pilot does and does not do | Explore one lab with an adult; do not imply account, cloud storage or a measured outcome. |

Publish at most one substantial post a week during the first review. Reuse the same accurate asset across Instagram and Facebook after checking each crop and caption. Consider TikTok only after reviewing the first two exports; do not pay to boost them during the usability cohort. Post time and platform choice are test variables, not established performance facts.

## Post 1 — water lab video

**Opening on screen**

- Arabic: ماذا لو أخطأ الحساس؟
- English: What if the sensor is wrong?
- French: Et si le capteur se trompait ?

**Arabic caption**

إذا أظهر الحساس ٦٠٪ بينما مستوى الماء الحقيقي ٩٦٪، هل تتوقف المضخة؟ في نموذجنا، حساس واحد لا يكفي. أضفنا حساسًا احتياطيًا واختبرنا القرار من جديد. جرّبوا المختبر معًا، واسألوا: ما الدليل الثاني الذي نحتاجه؟

أربعة مختبرات تفاعلية بالعربية والإنجليزية والفرنسية؛ بعض الصفحات ما زالت بالعربية. تجربة عائلية مجانية بإشراف بالغ.

**English caption**

If a sensor reads 60% while the true water level is 96%, should the pump stop? In our model, one sensor is not enough. We add a backup reading and test the decision again. Try the lab together and ask: what second piece of evidence would help?

Four interactive labs and the main family pages are available in Arabic, English and French. Free family exploration with an adult.

Quatre ateliers interactifs et les principales pages familiales sont disponibles en arabe, anglais et français. Découverte gratuite en famille, avec un adulte. Une relecture linguistique et un contrôle sur appareils réels restent nécessaires avant publication.

## Post 2 — parent prompt carousel

**Card sequence**

1. Before the test: “What do you think will happen?”
2. Try one change.
3. Look closely: “What happened?”
4. Explain together: “What changed, and what stayed the same?”

**Arabic caption**

في كل تجربة، اتركوا مساحة للتوقّع، ثم اختبروا فكرة واحدة، وبعدها تحدثوا عمّا حدث. لا توجد إجابة سريعة مطلوبة؛ يمكن أخذ استراحة أو التوقف في أي وقت.

**English caption**

Make room for a prediction, one test and a conversation about what happened. There is no need to rush to an answer; your family can pause or stop at any time.

**French caption**

Faites une place à une prédiction, à un test, puis à une discussion sur le résultat. Inutile de se presser vers une réponse ; la famille peut faire une pause ou s’arrêter à tout moment.

## Post 3 — routing lab video

**Opening on screen**

- Arabic: ماذا نغيّر إذا أُغلق الطريق؟
- English: What changes when a road closes?
- French: Que changer quand une route ferme ?

**Arabic caption**

الهدف أن تصل الشحنة إلى A أولًا. عندما يُغلق الطريق بين A وB، تتغير الخطة إلى A ثم C ثم B. ما الذي تغيّر؟ وما الذي حافظنا عليه؟ هذا نموذج مبسّط للتخطيط، وليس إرشادًا للملاحة على الطرق.

**English caption**

The delivery should reach A first. When the road between A and B closes, the plan changes to A, then C, then B. What changed, and what did we preserve? This is a simplified planning model, not real-world navigation advice.

**French caption**

La livraison doit arriver d’abord en A. Quand la route entre A et B ferme, le plan devient A, puis C, puis B. Qu’est-ce qui a changé et qu’avons-nous préservé ? Il s’agit d’un modèle simplifié, pas d’un conseil de navigation réel.

## Post 4 — transparent pilot invitation

**English caption**

BRKAR is a family space for learning through a test: predict, try an idea and explain what you noticed. Answers stay in the same browser profile, where other people using that profile may see them. There is no account, cloud backup or automated assessment. Explore one lab together with an adult.

**French caption**

BRKAR est un espace familial pour apprendre par l’expérience : prévoir, tester une idée et expliquer ce que l’on observe. Les réponses restent dans le même profil de navigateur et peuvent être vues par les personnes qui l’utilisent. Il n’y a ni compte, ni sauvegarde dans le cloud, ni évaluation automatisée. Essayez un atelier ensemble, avec un adulte.
