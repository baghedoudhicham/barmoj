# BRKAR market notes: 01TEK and Morocco support

Checked 10 October 2026. This is a working product and funding note, not a claim of competitor verification, legal eligibility, partnership, or funding approval. Recheck live programme terms before applying.

## 01TEK: a useful local reference, in a different lane

01TEK's current site leads with 01TEK Campus for private-school operators in Morocco. The offer centers on school administration: attendance, grades, fee collection, parent communications, school-specific deployment, and Massar import/export. It is sold through a demo-led, institution-facing journey and is presented in French, Arabic, and English. The site also describes AI teaching features and makes privacy/data-isolation claims; these are 01TEK's own product statements, not independently audited facts. Its homepage links to a TIME 2026 recognition claim; this note does not independently validate that recognition. [01TEK Campus](https://01tek.ma/fr) · [01TEK platform modules](https://01tek.ma/fr/erp-modules)

That makes 01TEK a strong local reference for audience clarity and concrete product proof, but not a direct substitute for BRKAR's intended family learning experience. BRKAR should keep its first audience as parents and children, and show the learner's actual process: notice, predict, test, explain, and revise. Its current examples—water safety, route planning, traffic flow, and fair rules—can anchor a distinct cognitive-skills proposition.

### Practical lessons for BRKAR

- Name the audience and the real-life problem immediately. 01TEK's first screen makes the school buyer and operational job legible; BRKAR should make a family learning moment equally concrete.
- Show the product doing its work. A sample child prompt, a safe experiment, and a parent reflection are stronger evidence than broad claims about “AI-ready” learning.
- Make Arabic, French, and English feel native in navigation and content, with the Arabic experience retaining its RTL hierarchy.
- Explain privacy in plain language and make every claim match implemented behavior. BRKAR should collect no child's name, school, avatar, answer, or profile for an adult interest list.
- Keep BRKAR's interaction pace deliberate. Avoid attention loops, streak pressure, rankings, engagement timers, or feeds designed to maximize screen time.
- Avoid claiming that BRKAR improves attention, intelligence, or learning outcomes until an appropriately designed pilot supports that claim.

## Morocco programmes to pursue or monitor

**Best near-stage question: Tamwilcom Innov Invest — Tech Start.** The official terms target a startup that has passed proof of concept and is developing a prototype or MVP, with application validation and an accompaniment agreement through a Tamwilcom partner. Funding covers up to 80% of eligible expenses, capped at MAD 200,000 (MAD 400,000 for a project with an invention patent); the remaining 20% must be contributed in an accepted form. The listed eligible expenses include product testing, services, staff, hosting, and marketing. The programme page provides terms, not a current call deadline, so BRKAR should ask a partner whether an intake is active and whether its product maturity and legal structure qualify. [Tech Start terms](https://www.tamwilstartups.ma/techStart.php) · [Innov Invest partners](https://tamwilstartups.ma/partenaires.php)

**Government Startup Venture Building.** The Ministry describes a lifecycle support offer with incubation and acceleration and non-dilutive instruments such as a life grant, incubation grant, honor loan, and seed loan; it explicitly names EdTech among target sectors for specialized startup support. This is a route into partner-led programmes, not a standing direct grant application. Ask Tamwilcom-listed operators about their next cohort, eligibility, location, founder-residency and incorporation conditions, and the exact amount and matching contribution before preparing a full application. [Ministry startup offer](https://www.mmsp.gov.ma/fr/nos-metiers/startups) · [Tamwilcom Venture Building](https://www.tamwilstartups.ma/presentationSVB.php)

**Later-stage: Tamwilcom Tech Boost.** This is a honor loan for startups beyond MVP seeking product-market fit. Official terms say up to 80% of eligible expenses, with a MAD 500,000 cap (MAD 750,000 for disruptive DeepTech), repayment over up to five years and a possible grace period up to two years. It requires partner validation and a 20% contribution, so it is more relevant after BRKAR has credible pilot evidence and a go-to-market plan. [Tech Boost terms](https://www.tamwilstartups.ma/techBoost.php)

## Readiness and application evidence

The strongest next application package is not an inflated impact story. It is a concise product demo, a milestone-and-budget plan, confirmed founder/entity eligibility, adult-supervised usability observations, curriculum and language review, a child-safeguarding and data-minimization plan, and a clear account of the founder contribution. Keep pilot evidence de-identified and adult-consented; do not send children's personal details or work samples to a public application page.

Before treating any instrument as available, ask a listed partner for written confirmation of the current intake, eligible legal form and founder residence, maturity threshold, eligible expense categories, matching contribution, application process, and whether the support is a grant, recoverable honor loan, or equity-linked instrument.
